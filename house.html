    >> TONIGHT // PAN-SEARED STEAK

    Serves 2 · about 25 minutes, plus resting

    You'll need

    • 2 steaks (ribeye or NY strip), 1 to 1½ inches thick
    • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
    • 1 tbsp oil that takes high heat (avocado or canola)
    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 3 cloves garlic, smashed
    • 2 sprigs thyme or rosemary
    • A cast-iron or heavy steel pan, and a meat thermometer

    Doneness

    Take it off atAfter resting
    Rare120°F125°F
    Medium-rare125–130°F130–135°F
    Medium135–140°F140–145°F
    Medium-well145–150°F150–155°F

    The USDA's safe minimum for whole cuts of beef is 145°F, rested 3 minutes.

    Steps

    1. Take the steaks out 30–45 minutes ahead. Pat them dry and season well with salt and pepper on every side.
    2. Heat the pan over high heat until it just starts to smoke, then add the oil.
    3. Lay the steaks in, away from you. Leave them alone for 3–4 minutes, until a deep brown crust forms.
    4. Flip them. Cook 2–3 minutes more, then turn the heat down to medium.
    5. Add the butter, garlic and herbs. Tilt the pan and spoon the foaming butter over the steaks for 1–2 minutes.
    6. Check the middle with the thermometer. Take them off about 5°F before where you want them to end up; they keep cooking as they rest.
    7. Rest on a board for 5–10 minutes, then slice against the grain and spoon the pan butter over the top.
